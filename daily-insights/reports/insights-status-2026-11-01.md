## Notes

- **Period Closed = Yes**: the period is over and was refreshed after it ended. A day closes when it is the day
  before yesterday or older; a month closes when it is older than the previous month, and the previous month
  (October) closes on the last Friday of November (27 November).
- **Period Closed = No** today: daily rows for 31 October and 1 November (Snapchat also 30 October: its 05:00 run
  was skipped while the backfill ran, so tomorrow's 05:00 run closes it); monthly rows for October and November.
- **No Data = Yes**: a month the platform returned nothing for (blank metrics). Daily rows are written only for days
  with impressions or spend, so no daily row has No Data = Yes.
